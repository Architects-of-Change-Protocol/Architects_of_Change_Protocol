Enterprise buyers now require policy-driven access, real approvals, and audit-ready evidence. Soberanía Enterprise delivers that entire lifecycle as a solution and a service, so your engineers stay focused on the product only they can build.

The Problem

Enterprise customers no longer accept broad, standing permissions. They expect access tied to policy, granted through approvals, scoped to a task, and expiring on its own — with every grant leaving evidence and every revocation provable. Most platforms were never designed for this. Access logic ends up scattered across services, approvals live in tickets, temporary permissions quietly become permanent, and audit requests turn into fire drills. None of it is impossible to build — but it competes for the same engineering hours as your core product, and the work never truly ends.

Why Not Build It Yourself

Generating a temporary credential takes an afternoon — that was never the hard part. The hard part is the rest of the lifecycle: mapping access to real policy, wiring approvals into how your teams actually work, guaranteeing expiry and revocation under load, producing evidence auditors accept, and keeping all of it correct as your product, teams, and regulations change. That isn't a feature; it's a discipline someone has to own indefinitely. Soberanía Enterprise owns it for you, on standards you can inspect — so the capability is there without the permanent headcount behind it.
